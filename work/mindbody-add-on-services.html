Add-on Services

The storefront inside Mindbody where a business discovers everything it can add, from a branded app to Capital, redesigned from four plain cards into a page that explains each one.

01  Overview

Everything a studio can add,
in one place.

Mindbody sells more than its core software. A business can add a branded app, a booking website, an insights dashboard, a listing in the Mindbody consumer app, payments, an AI front desk, funding through Capital, and new customers through ClassPass.

Add-on Services is where owners find all of it. In the live product in 2023 it was four look-alike cards tucked under Marketing in the navigation, each with a sentence and a button named after the product. I redesigned it as a storefront: a featured offer up top, every core add-on in one even grid, room for offers that need a picture, and the partner marketplace at the end.

02  The problem

Products the business
never knew it had.

Every card on the old page looked the same and every button just repeated the product name: Capital, Partners, Branded App, Branded Web. An owner skimming it learned what things were called, not what they would do for the business. And because the page lived under Marketing, products like Capital had nothing to do with where they were filed.

The list of things to offer was also growing. Mindbody announced it would acquire ClassPass in October 2021, and newer products like an AI front desk were on the way. Four equal cards would not hold that.

Sources: Mindbody to acquire ClassPass, October 2021

03  My role and how I worked

Giving a list of products
a hierarchy.

I designed the new page inside the platform's new navigation, working with product and engineering on which offers to feature and how the page could grow without another redesign.

Decision 01

Lead with the outcome, not the product name

Each card says what the add-on does for the business in plain words, like "Turn missed calls into sales and bookings", and ends with a clear Access link. The icon and color give every product a face owners can recognize the next time they see it elsewhere in Mindbody.

Decision 02

A storefront with a hierarchy

Not every product deserves the same weight at the same moment. A rotating banner carries the one offer to notice right now, the core products sit in an even grid, larger tiles with photography hold offers that need a picture to land, and the Partner Store closes the page for everything built by others.

04  The work

A page that can
keep growing.

05  Impact

One front door for
everything Mindbody sells.

Discovery owners can act on

Every add-on explains its value in a sentence and links straight to it, so the page answers why before it asks for a click.

A path into Capital

Add-on Services is one of the places owners meet Capital, and the featured banner gives it a prominent spot without crowding out the rest.

Room for what comes next

New products get a card in the grid or a tile with a picture, as Messenger and ClassPass show, without redesigning the page.
