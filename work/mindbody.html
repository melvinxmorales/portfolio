The software behind more than 41,000 fitness studios, salons and spas. I also redesigned Add-on Services, where owners discover everything Mindbody sells, and led the redesign of the branded app their clients book with.

01  How the work connects

Four projects,
one payments lifecycle.

Each payments project picks up where the last one ends. A business applies, takes payments at the front desk, defends them when a client disputes a charge, and grows with funding repaid from those same sales.

  1. 01 Apply

    Payment Processing

    Get approved to take card payments.

  2. 02 Collect

    Point of Sale

    Ring up a sale at the front desk.

  3. 03 Protect

    Disputes

    Answer a chargeback before the deadline.

  4. 04 Grow

    Capital

    Turn card sales into funding.
