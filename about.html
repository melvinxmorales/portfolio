How I work

Three habits that show up everywhere.

I design and build

Marcavo and Peelr are full products I shipped solo: database, API and interface. Understanding the whole stack makes every design decision sharper.

Systems, not screens

I designed both ends of one footwear business, Dardano's where you buy your shoes and EZ Shoe Repair where you get them fixed, plus design systems spanning iOS and Android.

Design for outcomes

Revenue, adoption, support load. Design is not done until it moves the business, and I frame decisions in those terms, not just user terms.
